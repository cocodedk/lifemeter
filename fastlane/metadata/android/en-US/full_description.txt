<p>LifeMeter turns your birthdate into a live dashboard of numbers about your life.</p>

<p>It shows the total number of days and seconds you have been alive, and a rough estimate of the food you have consumed over that time. It also runs a live count of global deaths and births that have occurred since you were born, plus a session counter for seconds, deaths, and births since you opened the app.</p>

<p>LifeMeter looks up your horoscope sign, its symbol, and its ruling planets from your birthdate. You set your birthdate once, and the app remembers it for every session after that.</p>
