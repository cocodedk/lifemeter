The numbers now rest on published data instead of guesses. World births and deaths come from the United Nations World Population Prospects 2024, year by year since 1950. Food is 1.7 kg a day, from the supply figures of the UN Food and Agriculture Organization, less waste. The sex estimate uses the average number of times a month at each age from the British Natsal-3 survey. The Seconds alive counter now includes the time since midnight.
